Field Agent Questions

Q: What happens if I get stuck on a decryption task?

A: Don't panic, Detective. Every decryption node comes with an "Request Intel" button. Click it to query the Oracle Assistant for a syntax hint specific to that challenge. Pay close attention to the Syntax Logs provided in the file.

Q: How do I save my progress?

A: The agency's mainframe doesn't automatically back up your memory logs yet. You must manually click the "SAVE" button in the top right of your Detective Dashboard before logging off, and "LOAD" when you return.

Q: Why are some leads restricted (🔒)?

A: You lack the clearance. You must solve the previous active lead to decrypt the necessary passcodes for the next one. The timeline is linear, Detective.

Q: What is the City Archives section?

A: The City Archives are permanently unlocked databanks containing general Python knowledge (Variables, Loops, Data Structures) that you can refer back to at any time during your investigation.
